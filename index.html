Issue #01 — From Beaches of Dunkirk to Your Wrist

On the Watch · 2 min read · 21 Jan 2026

The evacuation of Dunkirk is a remarkable war story, so compelling that it has inspired numerous cinematic adaptations over the last eight decades. A story about war heroes, a story of war tactics, but there is also a part to this story that isn't really about war, and that's the story of time under pressure. A story about minutes that decide lives: which makes calling a watch “Dunkirk” a huge responsibility. It’s not a name you get to play with, and it’s forced us to ask again and again: “Is it because it’s meaningful or because it’s attractive?”

And it certainly never allowed us to become theatrical or decorative. No faux-vintage, no romanticised military styling. Our Dunkirk Watch pays homage to the Miracle of Dunkirk, to the ship that saved the lives of 600 troops, and to the spirit of hope when everything is against you.

Aesthetics

If asked the question, “What did you leave out in design aesthetics?” our answer would be, “A lot of easy drama.” We could’ve added more overt military cues, more text, more symbolism, essentially turning this watch into a costume.

The Dunkirk Watch celebrates its firefighting heritage with quiet confidence, not visual shouting.

The watch features a bronze case with red painted pipes on the sides, honouring the engine and the pumping system of the Massey Shaw. In addition, the watch face is telegraph-styled, inspired by the twin telegraph of the Massey Shaw.

History on your wrist

The caseback of this watch is made from the melted 1940s Massey Shaw engine, so you are quite literally carrying an original piece of the Massey Shaw with you. The caseback also features a silhouette of the Massey Shaw and Operation Dynamo.

The heart of the watch

This is a serious watch, a calm watch, so the heart of it had to be something calm and trustworthy.

This watch features a Swiss-made Sellita SW220 movement with an accuracy of +/-4 seconds per day up to +/-15 seconds per day. This watch is about dependability under pressure. This watch doesn’t try to impress, but feels dependable, purposeful, and grounded.

As a brand, this watch says that we’re not interested in decoration without duty. That our watches start with values, and that objects should carry responsibility and not just style.
